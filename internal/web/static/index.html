FlexWSPR 

      
A WSPR receiver for FlexRadio. It opens its own panadapter and DAX IQ stream on the radio, records each two-minute cycle, decodes it with wsprd from WSJT-X, and can upload the spots to wsprnet.org.
        Web page: va3mw.github.io/FlexWSPR ·
        source and releases: github.com/va3mw/FlexWSPR

    


    

      

        
Getting started

        
    
          
  1. Install WSJT-X: FlexWSPR runs its wsprd decoder.
    2. 
          
  2. In Settings, enter your callsign and grid. Click Find radios and pick yours, or type its IP address.
    3. 
          
  3. Pick a receive antenna, by default or per band.
    4. 
          
  4. Tick Upload spots to wsprnet.org to report, then Save and restart.
    5. 
          
  5. Choose a band or a rotation in Schedule. The first spots come about two minutes after it connects.
    6. 
        

      

      

        
The tabs

        

          
Spots
The radio and band now, the next bands, every cycle and spot since start, and the best signal.

          
Openings
Which bands are open from your area, from wspr.live and from your own receiver, now and over 24 hours.

          
Schedule
One band, a band rotation all day, or bands by UTC time slot.

          
Settings
Station, radio, antennas, DAX IQ, Openings, decoder, files and the web page port.

          
Log
What FlexWSPR is doing, for troubleshooting.

        

      

      

        
Best signal score

        
The SNR a spot would have had from 1 W at 1000 km:

        
score = SNR − (dBm − 30) + 20·log10(km ÷ 1000)

        
More transmit power counts against a station, and each doubling of distance adds 6 dB. Distances under 100 km count as 100 km; spots with no grid or a hashed call are not scored.

      

      

        
Openings ratings

        
Paths with either end within the radius of your grid (500 km unless changed), in the last 30 minutes:

        
    
          
  • Quiet: no paths
    • 
          
  • Local: paths, but few over 1000 km
    • 
          
  • Open: 3+ paths over 1000 km, 10% or more of all paths
    • 
          
  • DX: 3+ paths over 5000 km, 5% or more of all paths
    • 
        

        
The Heard here rows are rated by the farthest station your own receiver heard.

      

      

        
If nothing decodes

        
    
          
  • Clock: WSPR needs the PC clock within about 1 second. On Windows, check with w32tm /query /status; on Linux, install chrony.
    • 
          
  • Antenna: check the antenna for the band in Settings.
    • 
          
  • DAX IQ channel: don't run another DAX IQ program, or a second FlexWSPR, on the same channel.
    • 
          
  • Still nothing: try Flip Q in .c2 files, then Swap I and Q.
    • 
          
  • The Log tab shows each cycle and any errors.
